Privacy Imprint

Privacy

In short: Omigator runs in your browser. It has no accounts, sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and has no server of its own that receives what you enter. Your browser talks directly to the services listed below, and only when you use the feature that needs them.

1. Controller

The person named in the imprint is responsible for this website.

2. Hosting

The public instance at omigator.rickinto.place is served by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. When you load the page, Vercel processes connection data (IP address, time, requested file, browser and operating system) to deliver the site and to protect it against abuse. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working, secure website). Vercel may process data in the USA; see Vercel's privacy policy. Fonts are delivered with the site; no requests go to Google or other font services.

3. Services your browser contacts

  • NCBI / National Library of Medicine (USA), when you search or screen: your search terms and GEO accession numbers, together with your IP address. Omigator fetches public dataset records (GEO) and abstracts (PubMed). NLM web policies.
  • OpenRouter, Inc. (USA), only if you enter an OpenRouter key: your key, your request text and checklist, and the public metadata of the datasets being screened. OpenRouter passes requests on to the model provider that serves the chosen model. OpenRouter's privacy policy.
  • Your own model endpoint, if you configure one: the same data as for OpenRouter, sent to the address you entered.

These transfers happen because you start them (search, draft, screen, explain). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The operator of this website receives none of this data.

4. Storage in your browser

Omigator keeps settings, your current protocol, your 👍/👎 marks and saved runs in your browser (localStorage, IndexedDB), and caches GEO records and model answers there to avoid paying twice. API keys are stored only if you tick “Remember keys on this device”. This data never leaves your device unless you share a link. You can delete it under Settings (cache), by removing saved runs, or by clearing the site data in your browser.

5. Share links

A share link contains the run itself after the “#”: protocol, GEO accessions, model probabilities, your marks and, if short enough, explanations. Browsers do not send this part of a link to any server. Anyone you give the link to can read its contents.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). As this website stores no personal data on servers of its own, requests mostly concern the hosting logs described in section 2.
